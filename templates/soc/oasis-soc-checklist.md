# OASIS-E Start of Care — Completion Checklist

> The OASIS SOC comprehensive assessment must be done by the assessing clinician
> **during an in-person visit** — it cannot be completed from records alone.
> Timeline: SOC visit within 48 hrs of referral/return home (or ordered date);
> assessment **completed within 5 calendar days after SOC** (M0090); export/transmit
> from Data Soft Logic per agency policy (within 30 days of M0090).

- Patient: ______________________ MRN: ________ SOC date (M0030): ________
- Assessing clinician/discipline: ______________________

## Administrative (pre-fill from DSL)
- ☐ Patient identifiers, payer, and demographics match the EMR
- ☐ M0080 discipline of assessing clinician
- ☐ M0090 date assessment completed (within 5 days of SOC)
- ☐ M0100 reason for assessment = Start of Care
- ☐ M0102/M0104 physician-ordered SOC date / referral date
- ☐ Inpatient discharge info & diagnoses entered (M1000-series)
- ☐ Primary & other diagnoses with symptom control ratings (M1021/M1023)

## Assessment domains (at the visit)
- ☐ Hearing, speech, vision (Section B)
- ☐ Cognition — BIMS, signs of delirium/CAM (Section C)
- ☐ Mood — PHQ-2→9, social isolation (Section D)
- ☐ Health conditions — pain effect on activity/sleep, dyspnea, fall history (Section J)
- ☐ Swallowing / nutritional approaches, height & weight (Section K)
- ☐ Skin — pressure ulcers/injuries by stage, stasis ulcers, surgical wounds (Section M)
- ☐ Respiratory, cardiac, elimination (UTI, incontinence/catheter, bowel)
- ☐ Neuro/emotional/behavioral status items
- ☐ Functional abilities & goals — GG0100 prior function, GG0110 prior device use,
  GG0130 self-care, GG0170 mobility (record usual performance, code discharge goals)
- ☐ ADL/IADL items (grooming, dressing, bathing, toileting, transferring, ambulation)
- ☐ Living arrangement / availability of assistance
- ☐ Health literacy, transportation, social determinants items (Section A/SDOH)

## Medications (must match `medication-reconciliation.md`)
- ☐ Drug regimen review completed (M2001) — issues → physician contacted by midnight
  next calendar day (M2003)
- ☐ High-risk drug classes (N0415)
- ☐ Medication management ability — oral (M2020) / injectable (M2030)

## Care management & plan
- ☐ Types/sources of assistance, caregiver ability & willingness
- ☐ Special treatments/procedures (O0110: O2, IV, chemo, dialysis, etc.)
- ☐ Influenza/COVID/pneumococcal vaccination items as applicable
- ☐ Plan of care synchronizes with physician orders (485) and stated goals
- ☐ Homebound status documented with supporting facts

## Finalize in Data Soft Logic
- ☐ All OASIS items answered — DSL scrubber/audit run, warnings resolved
- ☐ Assessment locked/signed by assessing clinician
- ☐ Exported/queued for transmission to iQIES per agency policy
- ☐ SOC packet (SOC template, med rec, route sheet) emailed to shared mailbox
