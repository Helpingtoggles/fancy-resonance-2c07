> Pre-fill Sections 1–6 from the Data Soft Logic EMR (intake/referral, demographics,
> insurance, physician orders, diagnoses, referral documents) **before** the visit.
> Sections 7–12 are completed during/after the in-person SOC visit.
> Completed form contains PHI — handle per agency HIPAA policy.
